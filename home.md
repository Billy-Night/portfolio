### World Surf Journal — Full-Stack Surf Trip Application

**Category:** Full-stack web application

World Surf Journal lets users register an account and save details about their surf trips. It is a CRUD application with a frontend, backend and database, supporting the creation, retrieval, updating and deletion of trips.

**Technologies:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express, MySQL.

- [Live World Surf Journal application](https://worldsurfjournal.netlify.app)
- [Frontend source code](https://github.com/Billy-Night/world_surf_journal)

### Weather Wise — Weather and Sports Application

**Category:** Frontend application

Weather Wise uses weather and location APIs to rate daily conditions for a user's selected sport and location. The rating is calculated using an algorithm based on relevant weather conditions.

**Technologies:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, OpenWeather API.

- [Live Weather Wise application](https://weatherwise.netlify.app/)
- [Source code](https://github.com/Billy-Night/weather_wise_app)

### TROWIT — Digital Business Card Application

**Category:** Client web application

TROWIT is a platform designed to simplify the exchange of personal and business contact information using digital business cards. Users can create accounts, create digital cards, connect with others through shareable URLs and order physical versions of their cards.

**Technologies:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express, MySQL.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a website cost?

Every project is different, but most websites start from around **€1,500**, but starter option can start from **700€**. The final cost depends on the number of pages, functionality, content requirements and integrations such as online booking or e-commerce.

### How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites take **4–8 weeks** from discovery to launch, depending on project complexity and how quickly content and feedback are provided.

### Do you work with businesses outside Capbreton and Hossegor?

Yes. While I'm based in Capbreton, I work with businesses throughout France and internationally.

### Will my website appear on Google?

Every website is built with SEO best practices in mind, including performance, accessibility, technical SEO and clear site structure. SEO is a long-term process, but the website is set up with a strong foundation.

### Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. I can help modernise a website through a visual refresh, improved performance, better SEO or new functionality.

### Do you provide maintenance?

Yes. Ongoing maintenance plans are available and can include updates, backups, security monitoring, performance checks and technical support.

### Can you add online booking or e-commerce?

Yes. I can integrate online booking systems, e-commerce platforms and third-party tools to meet business needs.

### Do I own my website?

Yes. Once the project is complete and paid for, you own the website and its content, and receive access to the tools needed to manage it.

### What do you need from me to get started?

An initial discussion about your business goals, any existing branding or content, and what you want your website to achieve.

### Can you help improve an existing website's SEO?

Yes. I can audit an existing website, identify opportunities and implement improvements to help increase visibility and attract more qualified traffic.
